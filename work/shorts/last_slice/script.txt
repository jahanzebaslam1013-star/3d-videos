You wake up with a note in your pocket.
It says: "Don't eat the last slice."
It's in your handwriting.
You laugh it off... and eat the last slice.
It was your roommate's.
Your roommate is a six-foot-five bodybuilder.
He doesn't yell. He just stares... and cracks his knuckles.
Next thing you know, everything hurts.
So you grab a pen, write yourself a warning,
slip it in your pocket... and fall asleep.
