You kick an old, dusty lamp.
You rub it. Poof. A genie.
"Three wishes," he says. "Choose wisely."
Wish number one: "Make me rich."
Ten million dollars hits your bank account.
Next morning, the tax office calls. They want half.
Then your cousins call. All forty-seven of them.
By Friday, you're broke again.
Wish number two: "Make me famous."
Now everybody knows your face.
Cameras outside your house. Fans in your bathroom.
You can't even buy milk without a selfie.
The genie floats over, smiling. "One wish left."
You think hard. Really hard.
"I wish... I'd never found this lamp."
Poof.
You're back on that empty street, walking home. And then...
