It's three in the morning.
You're on a boat… in the middle of the sea.
And the boat's engine… has died.
There are people everywhere. Four hundred people. On a boat that was built for one hundred.
In your pocket is a plastic bag. Inside it, your passport… and a photo of Ammi.
Someone asks: "How far is Italy?"
Nobody has an answer.
But this story didn't start here.
Eight months earlier. A small village in Punjab.
Your name is Hamza. Age twenty-four.
You have a BA degree… and no job.
For two years you've been sending out CVs. Replies? Zero.
Abba ji has a small shop. And the household expenses… go up every month.
Then one day… your phone buzzes.
A new photo of Bilal on Instagram. Your cousin. In Italy.
Black sunglasses. A red car. Caption: "Life set hai yaar."
You stare at the photo for a long time.
Two years ago, Bilal was in this same village. Just like you.
The next day, at the chai dhaba… you meet "Agent bhai".
He smiles and says: "Just reach Europe once… then everything's set."
"How much will it cost?"
"Twenty-five lakh. Straight to Italy. Full guarantee."
Twenty-five lakh. You've never seen that much money in your life.
That night, you talk to Abba ji.
He stays quiet for a long time.
Then he says only this: "We'll sell the land."
Ammi brings out the gold from her dowry.
Nobody at home says anything. But everyone understands… now there's no way back.
A week later, you're at the airport. For the first time in your life.
Ammi has packed parathas in your bag. And your old sweater.
First stop: Dubai. Everything seems fine.
Then another country. Then another. In every place, a new "agent".
And every agent says the same thing: "Just a little more."
Then you reach Libya.
And here… the "full guarantee" ends.
You're taken to a warehouse.
One room. Eighty people. One bathroom.
On the very first day, your phone is taken away.
Food once a day. Sometimes not even that.
Here you meet Saleem chacha. Fifty years old. From Sialkot.
This is his third attempt.
"Beta, the first two times… the boat came back."
"Then why try again?" you ask.
He smiles faintly: "What would I do if I went back? The debt is there too."
Three weeks pass.
Then one night the agent comes. You get a phone… for just two minutes.
"Five lakh more for the boat. Tell your family."
You call Abba ji.
And you lie: "Abba ji, everything's fine. Just a little more money."
On the other end… a long silence.
"All right, beta. It'll be done."
You don't know that he has mortgaged the shop as well.
Two minutes are up. Phone taken back.
One night, without any warning, everyone is woken up. "Move! Quick!"
A truck. Then darkness. Then the seashore.
And standing there… is the boat.
Old. Rusted. And very, very small.
Some people stop. But they're pushed from behind.
You're sent below. Inside the boat. Where there's little air… and no light.
Saleem chacha is with you.
Day one: people are singing. "Italy! Italy!"
Day two: the water starts running out.
People start arguing over every sip.
Day three… the engine dies.
Now you're right where this story began.
Three in the morning. Four hundred people. And nothing but water all around.
Saleem chacha holds your hand. "Beta, think of your family. It gives you strength."
You take out Ammi's photo. The plastic bag is wet now.
Then in the distance… a light. A huge ship.
Everyone shouts. Waves their hands. Swings their shirts in the air.
And the ship… sails on past.
Nobody says a word.
Before dawn, the wind picks up. The waves rise.
The boat tilts to one side. Then further.
And then… everything goes dark.
You open your eyes.
A blanket. A tent. And a voice… that doesn't speak your language.
You survived.
In a shaking voice you ask: "Saleem chacha?"
Nobody answers.
Of the four hundred people on your boat that night… only a few dozen survived.
In the camp there's a long line. For a phone call.
You call Abba ji.
When he hears your voice… he breaks down. For the first time in your life, you hear him cry.
"Beta, the land is gone, the shop is gone… it doesn't matter. You're alive. Just come back."
Two days later, in the camp, you meet a young man. Pakistani. He's been here three years.
You recognise him.
Bilal.
He's not in Italy. He never even got there.
He works at a car wash. Sometimes. Without papers.
"And that red car?" you ask.
He looks down. "It was a customer's. I stood next to it for two minutes for the photo."
"What would I tell my family? That I sold everything… and got nothing?"
You don't say anything. Because you understand.
That night Bilal gives you his phone. "Send your family a photo. It'll put their minds at ease."
You stand near the camp gate. A car is passing by.
You smile. Click.
Thousands of miles away… in that same village in Punjab…
a boy is looking at your photo.
And thinking… "Hamza bhai's life is set."
The next day, at the chai dhaba… Agent bhai is smiling.
According to the FIA, from June 2023 to April 2026, at least 335 Pakistanis… lost their lives on this same journey.
Behind every photo, there's a story.
And some stories… never get posted.
