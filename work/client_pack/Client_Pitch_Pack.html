How every project works (tell clients this)

1. BriefThe client sends a topic or script, the target platform, length, language and 1–2 reference videos.
2. ScriptWe write or polish the script (hook, escalation, twist), and the client approves it.
3. VoiceAI narrator (English or Urdu) or the client's own voiceover. The client approves a sample.
4. FramesWe send a storyboard of real frames before the full render, so fixes are cheap here.
5. RenderFull animation, captions, music and SFX, loudness-matched for YouTube and TikTok.
6. DeliverMP4 (1080×1920 or 1920×1080), plus a thumbnail and project files if they were in the package.

Stickman POV Animated Shorts

Hand-drawn stickman “POV” Shorts for TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Reels (9:16, 30–90 s)
Real frames from our stickman Shorts pipeline (1080×1920), shown with captions on.

Vertical 30–90 second Shorts in a hand-drawn stickman style. They have rough “boiling” black outlines, flat muted colours and big expressive anime-style heads. Bold text pops in, along with red rubber stamps (FIRED, APPROVED, NO WAY BACK), speech bubbles, film grain and slow camera push-ins. Each one has a deep narrator voiceover, word-timed captions, a music bed and a sound effect on every action. Stories are told as “POV: You…” so the viewer is the main character, which is why people watch to the end. This is ideal for faceless channels that need a steady supply of Shorts.

Rules for taking jobs

  • Long-form and Blender jobs taken off-platform (LinkedIn or direct): 50% upfront, 50% on delivery.
  • Always confirm the script and voice before quoting a delivery date. Delivery time starts after approval.
  • Platform fees: Fiverr keeps 20%, and Upwork's fee varies (about 0–15%). The prices here are list prices, so keep them.
